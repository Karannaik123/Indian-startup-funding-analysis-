 🇮🇳 Indian Startup Funding Intelligence Dashboard

 Overview
An end-to-end data analytics project analysing 3,000+ startup funding deals 
in India (2015–2020) to uncover sector trends, investor patterns, and 
geographic hotspots across the Indian startup ecosystem.

 🔗 Live Dashboard
👉 [View Interactive Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/karan.naik1700/viz/IndianstartupFundingIntelligence/IndianstartupFundingIntelligence?publish=yes)

 📊 Key Findings
- **Consumer Internet** dominates with 935 deals — India's most funded sector
- **Bangalore** leads all cities with $11.3B in total funding
- **Westbridge Capital** is the top investor by funding amount ($3.9B)
- Startup funding **peaked in 2017** at $10.4B before a market correction
- **Flipkart** raised the most funding of any single startup ($4B+)

 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Python** (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn) — data cleaning & analysis
- **SQL** — business queries and data exploration
- **Tableau Public** — interactive dashboard
  

 💡 Business Insights
1. Consumer Internet and Technology together account for 60%+ of all deals
2. Bangalore and Mumbai are the clear startup hubs — tier 2 cities largely absent
3. 2016-2017 was India's peak funding era driven by large unicorn rounds
4. Softbank and Westbridge Capital dominate large ticket investments
5. Data quality issues (city name inconsistencies) reflect real-world analytics challenges
